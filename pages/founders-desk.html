Founder's Desk
Vision, Creativity, and Leadership

Anurudha Vaishali Avinash Gangurde

Founder & Creative Director, VAVI STUDIOS

Creativity is more than a profession for Anurudha Vaishali Avinash Gangurde—it is a way of preserving emotions, shaping brands, and transforming ideas into timeless visual experiences.

As the Founder and Creative Director of VAVI STUDIOS, Anurudha has spent over five years building one of Nashik's emerging creative production houses, delivering premium photography, filmmaking, video production, and branding solutions for individuals, businesses, and organizations.

Armed with an MBA in Information Technology, a Diploma in Video Production, and multiple professional certifications in Photography, Cinematography, Graphic Design, Digital Marketing, Video Editing, Branding, and Creative Media, he combines strategic thinking with artistic vision to create content that is both visually compelling and commercially effective.

Throughout his journey, Anurudha has successfully led 70+ weddings, produced commercial campaigns, corporate films, product advertisements, fashion editorials, portraits, and digital marketing projects. His work reflects a commitment to authenticity, cinematic storytelling, and meticulous attention to detail, ensuring every project leaves a lasting impact.
